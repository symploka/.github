<h2>⚡ WHAT IS SYMPLOKA?</h2>

<p>
SYMPLOKA is building a modern ecosystem for creating, launching,
operating and protecting things on the internet.
</p>

<p>

the goal is to bring the important pieces closer together.
</p>

<h3>
